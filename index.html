About the Event

Objective

Placement Premier League (PPL) is a placement preparation competition designed to simulate real placement rounds and enhance students' aptitude, communication, technical, analytical, and interview skills.

The Challenge

Participants compete through multiple elimination rounds including aptitude, memory sharpening, group discussion, technical quiz, and HR interview rounds.
